Import a CV you already have as text

Paste the plain-text format — the one content-example.txt uses: your name, your role, then sections underlined with =. Czech and English section headings are both understood. Nothing is sent anywhere except to this app, to be turned into form fields you can then correct.

A PDF only says where the letters sit on the page, not what they mean, so reading one back is recovery rather than conversion. The text lands in the box above for you to fix before it becomes a CV. A scanned PDF — a picture of a CV rather than text — cannot be read at all.

Nothing you type is saved on the server. Export JSON keeps a copy on your own computer; import it later to carry on.
